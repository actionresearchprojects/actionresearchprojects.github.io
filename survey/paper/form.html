House 5 Comfort Survey Utafiti wa Hali ya Hewa Ndani ya Nyumba

ARC / Children's Ecovillage  ·  One sheet per girl Karatasi moja kwa kila msichana
Instructions for the surveyor / Maelekezo kwa anayefanya utafiti
Take the girls aside one by one. All sections are filled in by an adult, not by the girl. Sections B and C are questions to ask her. Complete once a month, on a Saturday morning. She must have been in this room for at least 30 minutes before you start. If she has only just come in, wait until 30 minutes have passed.
Please ensure that photographs of these sheets are taken and sent to arc.archie@simmondsmills.com once a month.
Mchukue kila msichana peke yake, mmoja baada ya mwingine. Sehemu zote zinajazwa na mtu mzima, si msichana. Sehemu B na C ni maswali ya kumuuliza. Jaza mara moja kwa mwezi, asubuhi ya Jumamosi. Lazima awe amekuwa katika chumba hiki kwa angalau dakika 30 kabla hujaanza. Kama ameingia hivi punde, subiri hadi dakika 30 zipite. Tafadhali hakikisha picha za karatasi hizi zinapigwa na kutumwa kwa arc.archie@simmondsmills.com mara moja kwa mwezi.

SECTION D   MOSQUITOES    SEHEMU D   MBU
These questions help us work out where mosquitoes are getting into the house, even though the insect mesh is fitted. Ask her about what she notices generally, not only last night.
Maswali haya yanatusaidia kujua mbu wanaingiaje ndani ya nyumba, hata ingawa nyavu za wadudu zimewekwa. Muulize kuhusu anachokiona kwa kawaida, si usiku uliopita tu.
