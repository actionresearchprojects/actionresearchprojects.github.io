    Introduction¶

    The ARC expert group's current understanding of key technical aspects of thermal comfort and wellbeing, and ARC's building performance standard for minimising occupant overheating in hot, and hot and humid, climates.

    This document sets out the ARC expert group's current understanding of various key technical aspects of thermal comfort and wellbeing. It includes our thinking on how to increase building performance resilience in the face of intermittent power supply and problems related to the cost and availability of key building materials and mechanical services equipment.

    It outlines ARC's building performance standard for minimising occupant overheating in buildings in hot, and hot and humid climates.

    The ARC Cool Buildings Standard is conceived as being helpful for countries in climate-vulnerable regions, where there exist challenging socio-economic conditions that do not favour easily delivering new and refurbished buildings to the Passivhaus Standard in one step.

    The document should also be used as reference for those using ARC's educational materials.

    As our understanding and experience increases, this document will be updated - please regularly check that you are referencing the latest version.
